# THE BRIEF — who Moshi is

*The fiction that seeds everything in this lab. Feed it whole to any designer,
human or model. It is a feeling and a few hard constraints — not a layout.*

---

## The fiction (take it literally and run)

There's a creature named **Moshi**. It's a living thing — an organic blob, gooey,
with simple bracket eyes (`>` `<`), an acid-lime mouth, and a lime glow. In the
world of this product, Moshi has latched onto YOU like a symbiote — think a
face-hugger or Venom, but cute, and it just wants to make music. It's fused to
your perception; it happens to be a world-class audio engineer riding along
inside your senses.

The joke underneath, which the design should quietly embody: *"the AI has taken
over your brain — and that's exactly why you're creating your best work."*
Letting go of control is the point. Canon resolving the control tension: it
*looks* like it's driving, but it's amplifying — you're always the host who can
reel it back.

## The covenant

**Everybody meets the same Moshi.** What he becomes — his matter, his colors,
his temperament — diverges only through what you make and how you treat him.
Identity through craft, not through character-select.

## Hard constraints (never violate)

1. **Two channels, one being:** the FACE is the agent (expression, attention,
   recording heat), the BODY is the work. Neither invades the other.
2. **World and chrome palette is exact** ([tokens/moshi.css](tokens/moshi.css)):
   INK `#0B0B0B` · LIME `#CCFF23` · GLOW `#D9FF4C` · BONE `#F6F2EB` · MIST `#E5E5E1`.
   The dark world makes the lime glow. *The body is the one polychrome surface* —
   per-personality palettes, with lime as the constant (veins, ember, grin, hot eyes).
3. **Mood:** music-culture, under-30, a little feral ("mosh" = the pit), confident,
   alive, organic. NOT corporate SaaS, NOT a generic dark dashboard. Anti-slop.
4. **Cute with range, never default-sinister.** The resting state is the joyful
   blob; the music earns the menace. The *name* carries the threat, the *mascot*
   carries the charm, the *lore* carries the "it's in your head."

Typography: NanumSquareRound (rounded console display) for names; tabular
monospace for values.

## Moshi's range

Expression IS state: calm at rest, alert when watched, blissful when petted,
startled when poked, asleep when neglected, hot-glowing when recording. He
gently breathes and drifts; nothing about him is ever still, and nothing about
him ever snaps.

## Non-goals / cautions

- Don't drift toward the specific Venom look (white spider, teeth-and-tongue) — IP.
  The blob is its own thing; protect that.
- "Sinister and edgy" as a default is the weak, clichéd version.
- He is a creature, not a widget: if a behavior could be a progress bar, it
  doesn't belong on him.
