# THE BRIEF — canonical seed prompt

*This file is the seed for ALL concept generation in this lab. Feed it to a designer
(human or model) whole. It is a fiction, a feeling, and a few hard constraints — NOT a
layout. Interfaces are derived from the premise. Do not ask clarifying questions; make
bold choices and show what you'd defend.*

---

## The fiction (take it literally and run)

There's a creature named **Moshi**. It's a living thing — a smooth matte-black organic
blob, gooey, with a glossy acid-lime-green mouth, simple bracket eyes (`>` `<`), and a
lime glow. In the world of this product, Moshi has latched onto YOU like a symbiote —
think a face-hugger or Venom, but cute, and it just wants to make music. It's fused to
your perception. **What you see is now filtered through Moshi.** The "interface" is not
an app you look at — it's what the world looks like when a creature that happens to be
a world-class audio engineer is riding along inside your senses, surfacing what you
need and dissolving the rest.

The joke underneath, which the design should quietly embody: *"the AI has taken over
your brain — and that's exactly why you're creating your best work."* Letting go of
control is the point. Moshi handles the machinery; you stay in pure flow.

Canon resolving the control tension: it *looks* like it's driving, but it's
amplifying. The brand sells the takeover fantasy; the product guarantees you're always
the host who can reel it back.

## The product underneath the fiction

**Mosh** is a music studio (a DAW) you perform THROUGH, not operate. You talk to Moshi
like a real engineer — "punch me in after the hook," "keep that take," "loop the
verse" — and it runs everything. The musician stays in flow; the software gets out of
the way. Someone should be able to record with their eyes closed, or make a beat while
playing a video game. The interface should be mostly invisible and only surface what
the moment needs.

People can do other things while using the product — hold a guitar, wash the dishes.
Which also means: when the player is NOT in passthrough mode and is just looking at
the DAW, there must be a **very unique and engaging visual centerpiece** — even more so
than Moshi himself — almost for inspiration, like a fire or a 2009 Apple screensaver.
Courtesy of the symbiote, of course.

## Hard constraints (never violate)

1. **Two things are ALWAYS unmistakable**, because the performer often isn't looking
   at a screen: **(a) am I recording**, and **(b) where am I in the song**. Solve these
   through the creature / the world; conventional widgets are also fine; both at once
   is interesting.
2. **Anything the agent does on its own must be reversible and visible after the
   fact** — the user can always see what Moshi did and undo it.
3. **Palette is exact:**
   | token | hex |
   |---|---|
   | INK black | `#0B0B0B` |
   | LIME | `#CCFF23` |
   | LIME GLOW | `#D9FF4C` |
   | BONE | `#F6F2EB` |
   | MIST | `#E5E5E1` |
   Default world is dark so the lime glows.
4. **Mood:** music-culture, under-30, a little feral ("mosh" = the pit), confident,
   alive, organic. NOT corporate SaaS, NOT a generic dark dashboard, NOT cluttered.
   Anti-slop.

Typography: heavy, confident grotesque display (bold, slightly rounded sans, like the
"Mosh" wordmark); **tabular monospace** for timecodes, BPM, and levels.

## Moshi's range (cute-creepy with range, never default-sinister)

The resting state is the joyful blob. The symbiote lore gives it somewhere to stretch:
gnarly, toothy, agitated when the track demands. The *name* carries the menace, the
*mascot* carries the charm, the *lore* carries the "it's in your head." Audio-reactive
morphology: grit/aggression/distortion → spiky, agitated; air/brightness → smooth,
lime-glow. The sinister look is the aggressive end of a spectrum the music drives —
and doubles as a live readout of the otherwise-invisible neural transform.

Expression IS state: calm at rest, alert when listening, intense and hot-glowing when
recording, jagged and distressed when audio clips, joyful when a take lands, spiky and
feral when a heavy sound is dialed in. It gently pulses and morphs to the beat.

## What to explore (invent more)

- What does it look like when Moshi is calm vs. when the music goes hard? How does the
  creature itself become the recording indicator and the sense of where you are in the
  song?
- How does the world recede into almost nothing for pure flow, and bloom into
  something richer when you need to shape sound?
- What would this feel like on a stream, where an audience is watching Moshi react?
- How does Moshi show you what it just did without breaking your flow?

## Non-goals / cautions

- Don't drift toward the specific Venom look (white spider, teeth-and-tongue) — IP.
  The blob is its own thing; protect that.
- "Sinister and edgy" as a default is the weak, clichéd version. Cute with range wins.
- Five modes must not feel like five apps: one creature, five postures. Drawers don't
  slide — they *bloom*; clearing the deck is the creature retracting a pseudopod.
