# 002 · SPECIMEN — the morphology spectrum

**Thesis.** Moshi's body is the instrument cluster. Every state the performer must
feel-without-looking is carried by the creature's physiology, so one glance — or one
blurry peripheral impression — tells you everything: REC is *heat* (an ember glowing
through the matte skin, pulsing ~1.1 Hz), song position is a *lume circling the
membrane* once per 8 bars, clipping is a *full-body tremor* with ×× eyes, and the
Color-Rack axes drive arousal (spike amplitude/frequency) — calm blob → feral urchin.

**The fiction, literally.** A symbiote doesn't show you a dashboard; you read its body
the way you read a dog. The aggressive look is not the default skin — it's the hot end
of a spectrum *the music drives*, which doubles as the only honest readout of what the
neural transform is doing.

**The two non-negotiables.**
- *Am I recording:* the body IS the record light. Heat is unmistakable at any distance,
  any blur, eyes half-closed.
- *Where am I:* the orbiting lume — position as a place on a body, not a number. (`BAR
  x.y` mono stays in the corner for the skeptic.)

**Feel.** Cute at rest, genuinely a little unsettling at FERAL — exactly the
cute-creepy-with-range the brand wants.

**Risks.** Raymarching cost (kept at 1.25× DPR cap here); eye/face art direction is
DOM-overlaid in the study and would be in-surface in production.

**Steal even if killed.** Heat-as-REC and the membrane lume are the strongest answers
to the two non-negotiables in the whole wave; port them into ANY winning direction.
