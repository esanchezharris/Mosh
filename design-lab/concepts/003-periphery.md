# 003 · PERIPHERY — the vision filter

**Thesis.** Take "fused to your perception" at face value: the interface lives where a
symbiote would actually sit — at the *edges of vision*. The center of the screen is
permanently yours (game, lyrics, nothing). The studio is a membrane of ink flesh
creeping in from the screen border, and it only ever creeps — it never covers.

**The fiction, literally.** Things at the corner of your eye are felt, not read. Moshi
surfaces what the moment needs as peripheral sensation: a hot rim, a traveling bead,
a whispered chip — and dissolves the rest.

**The two non-negotiables.**
- *Am I recording:* the ENTIRE periphery runs hot and breathes at 1.1 Hz. You cannot
  miss it even with your eyes on a game — it's literally everywhere you aren't looking.
- *Where am I:* a lume bead orbits the screen border, one lap = the song, 8 ticks =
  sections. Position becomes geography: "the bead is on the right edge" = mid-song.

**Agent visibility.** Deeds materialize as glyph chips (top-right), readable after the
fact, × to undo, then dissolve. The whisper line (bottom-center) is the only input.

**Feel.** The most radical "record while gaming" surface — the product's founding
scenario made literal. Calm to the point of invisibility, then unmistakable when hot.

**Risks.** Discoverability (everything is summonable, nothing is visible) — needs the
membrane to *bloom inward* on summon (next iteration). Edge-glow must survive OS-level
overscan/rounded corners.

**Steal even if killed.** The perimeter-lap position metaphor; periphery-as-REC. Both
work composited onto ANY other direction, including the current production UI.
