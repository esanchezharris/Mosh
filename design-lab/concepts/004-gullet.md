# 004 · THE GULLET — creature as timeline

**Thesis.** The one sacred visual of every DAW — the timeline — re-derived from the
lore: the song is something Moshi is *digesting*. Its body stretches across the screen,
segmented into sections like a grub; takes and clips are glowing lumps under the
translucent skin; the playhead is a peristaltic wave you can see (and would feel)
traveling left to right; the mouth at the right end swallows the live signal while
recording. Undo is regurgitation — gross, joyful, exactly "mosh."

**The fiction, literally.** Audio engineers say "the song eats the take" — here it
actually does. Lineage is physical: what the creature swallowed is visibly *in* it.

**The two non-negotiables.**
- *Where am I:* the bulge. A traveling deformation of a body is pre-attentively
  trackable in a way a thin playhead line never was — plus the current section label
  burns lime above the skin.
- *Am I recording:* the mouth is open, glowing, and visibly swallowing particles; the
  room's edges run faintly hot. Stop recording and the swallow completes into a lump.

**Feel.** The most character-driven arrangement view conceivable — and weirdly
*informative*: section constrictions give the song silhouette at a glance.

**Risks.** Scale — does it survive 64 tracks and dense edits? (Probable answer: the
GULLET is the *performance* view, and blooms open into lanes on demand — "one creature,
five postures.") Also flirts with cute-overload; restraint in the lump count helps.

**Steal even if killed.** Peristaltic playhead; swallow-on-record; regurgitate-on-undo
as the canonical undo animation everywhere in the product.
