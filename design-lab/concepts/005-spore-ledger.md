# 005 · SPORE LEDGER — visible metabolism

**Thesis.** Stake the whole direction on hard-constraint #2: *anything the agent does
on its own must be reversible and visible after the fact.* In the lore, that's
metabolism. Every autonomous action buds off Moshi's body as a living cell — a spore —
that drifts into a ledger column. Hover a cell and the deed is written on it ("✓ kept
take 4"); pop it and the deed is undone in a burst of lime ink. Old deeds slowly fade —
metabolized — but the stack is real underneath.

**The fiction, literally.** You can't see inside a tool, but you can watch an organism
eat. Trust in an autonomous engineer comes from *seeing its digestion*, not from a
settings page promising reversibility.

**The two non-negotiables.** Deliberately solved *quietly* here (a pulsing ember + a
position ring at top-center, conventional and small) — this direction spends its
boldness budget entirely on agent-action visibility. That's the point of having
multiple directions: each stresses one wall of the design space.

**Feel.** The most *trust-building* of the wave. Watching deeds accumulate is oddly
calming — the "it's working for me" feeling productized.

**Risks.** DOM/SVG-filter goo is the least Avalonia-portable rendering in Wave 1
(flagged per house rule 5); production would redo the goo in Skia. Cell column must
never become a notification feed — cap at 7, fade, no sounds.

**Steal even if killed.** Pop-to-undo as the universal gesture for agent actions;
the budding animation (deed physically *comes from* the creature — provenance you
can see).
