# 007 · TERRARIUM — the specimen, kept quietly

**Thesis.** Wave-2 synthesis, by request: SPECIMEN's reactivity × SPORE LEDGER's
out-of-the-way humility, with HEARTH demoted from centerpiece to atmosphere. The
raymarched body keeps *all* of its instrument-cluster honesty — recording is still
an ember under the skin, the song position still circles the membrane, undo still
makes it shudder — but it now lives at SPORE LEDGER's scale and station: a corner
resident, not a centerpiece. The middle of the screen belongs to you.

**What was kept from each parent.**
- **002 SPECIMEN** — the SDF body verbatim (lobes, breathing, noise skin), heat,
  mouth, clip tremor, membrane lume. The state *deck* is gone: reactivity is now
  driven by real events (focus = listening, R = recording, deed = exhale,
  pop = flinch, cursor proximity = it perks up).
- **005 SPORE LEDGER** — the entire layout grammar: vitals top-center, ledger
  column right, command line bottom, creature bottom-left. Deeds still bud off
  the body and fly to the ledger; pop-to-undo unchanged.
- **001 HEARTH** — the domain-warped ink field as a *background organ*: clock
  slowed ~2×, two vein iso-lines instead of three, heat ceiling roughly halved,
  vignette deepened, and the onset pressure rings **removed entirely**. It only
  warms sympathetically when the creature works (couples to heat/arousal).

**The fiction, literally.** A terrarium: the organism is alive and legible through
the glass, the substrate glows faintly because something lives in it, and you get
on with your day. Observation without occupation.

**Feel.** The calmest of the wave that still reads as *alive*. The test: can you
leave it on a second monitor for an hour and never resent it, yet still catch a
punch-in from the corner of your eye?

**Risks.** Two WebGL contexts (field + creature) — fine here since the raymarch
canvas is tiny, but production should composite in one pass. The corner body at
210px must stay readable when feral; if arousal detail mushes at that scale,
clamp the noise frequency below SPECIMEN's ceiling.

**Steal even if killed.** Background-as-sympathetic-organ (the room warms when
the agent works); event-driven creature states replacing demo decks.
