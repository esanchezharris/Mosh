# 011 · THE SCENE — everyone's session, at a glance

*v2 of the battle-mode concept. The v1 hourglass "looked really bad" (user, 2026-06-10)
and 1v1 framing was wrong: "you're almost never in a 1v1 — maybe your collaborators are
on one side of your topbar and you can see the orbs of all your competitors on the
other side. A small representation of what other people are doing."*

## Thesis

Presence is matter at small scale. The topbar carries two rails of **dithered orbs**
(2D-canvas, Bayer-checker banding, on-twos wobble — zero extra GL contexts):

| rail | who | what you see |
|---|---|---|
| **CREW** (left) | collaborators in your session | full detail: lime rim, ember heart while they record, deed ticks popping ("+clip", "comped", "rerolled") as they work |
| **THE SCENE** (right) | rivals / the wider scene | **fog orbs** — cold grey-banded spheres whose SIZE is how built their session is. No detail. No sound. |

**Tap a rival orb → PEEK**: their artifact replaces yours on the stage for four seconds,
rendered in presence-in-fog (silhouette + scale, no shards, no fringe, no lime) under
the line *"YOU CAN SEE IT · YOU CAN'T HEAR IT."* The trash-talk economy survives; the
audio never leaks.

Your own session stays the centerpiece: the pit-grammar artifact (chill v6 defaults),
REC heat, beat slams, and the always-present transport + BEAT/VOX lanes below.

## What died, what survived from THE WAGER

- **Dead:** the eroding hourglass (bad-looking), the W/L round flow, the 1v1 stage.
- **Survived:** presence-in-fog as the privacy boundary (the user's favorite), the
  build-vs-build legibility (orb size = artifact size), judge-by-ear ethos.

## What to steal even if multiplayer waits

- The orb grammar generalizes: any agent/process/collaborator can live in the topbar
  as a small matter-readout (a render job as a growing orb; Moshi's job queue).
- PEEK = the universal "glance at someone's state without joining it" gesture.
