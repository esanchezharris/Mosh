# 011 · THE WAGER — battle mode: build vs the hourglass

*Seeded directly by the user (2026-06-10): "I noticed there's a kind of a cool
choking/particle/shrinking effect when we hit record — it might be cool in competitive
games if there was an artifact you were building, as well as an artifact dwindling away —
your timer/hourglass — that also let you know what your opponents were up to without
hearing it."*

## Thesis

Mosh's matter-grammar (009: the song as a stone that grows as you build) makes
competitive music modes legible **without leaking audio**. Everything you'd want a
battle HUD to say is said by three bodies in one raymarched scene:

| body | says | grammar |
|---|---|---|
| **YOU** (left) | how built your entry is | the pit's artifact: grows with every drop, earns shards/light, fringe when the vocals land |
| **THE GLASS** (center) | time remaining, viscerally | an hourglass that is *eaten* — erodes top-down, sheds lime sand, the waste-line burns, final-15s = hot + trembling + bouncing countdown |
| **THEM** (right) | what the rival is up to | **presence-in-fog** (field-notes motif): full silhouette + size, zero detail, zero lime — you watch it swell, you never hear it |

End of round: a rotated block-shadowed **W/L stamp** (pure PS2 victory screen), the
comparison line ("YOUR ARTIFACT 84% · THEIRS 71% — NOW JUDGE BY EAR"), reset. The
stamp is provisional on purpose: the artifact sizes seed the trash-talk; ears decide.

## How it meets the constraints

- **Am-I-recording / where-am-I**: the round clock is the dominant object in the room;
  REC-armed chip in the strip; the playhead runs the mini-arrangement.
- **DAW controls always present**: compact transport + BEAT/VOX lanes at the bottom —
  drops land as real clips. It's a music tool wearing a game mode, not vice versa.
- **One GL context** for all three bodies (context discipline, HOUSE_STYLE v2.1).
- **House style v2 + the signal chain**: facets, dither bands, on-twos, chunky chrome,
  whole page through the PS2 cable.

## What to steal even if battle mode never ships

- The eroding-matter timer (deadline = a thing being eaten) for ANY long render/export.
- Fog-silhouette rendering for any "collaborator presence without content" surface
  (async multiplayer lurk mode, A&R preview, unreleased-stem placeholder).
- The W stamp entrance (`stomp` keyframe) for any hard-won state change.
