# Field notes — the inspiration pass, actually looked at

*2026-06-09. 100 of the 101 linked X posts fetched as real imagery (fxtwitter/vxtwitter
APIs — see [refs-fetch.py](refs-fetch.py); thumbnails live in `refs/`, gitignored,
regenerate any time). ~50 studied closely. These are notes from LOOKING, not from
author bios. [INDEX.md](INDEX.md) is the catalog; this is the reading.*

The collection isn't random — squint and it's one taste: **soft matter rendered with
optical honesty, on voids, annotated like lab equipment.** Eight motifs, each with
what to steal.

---

## 1. The chromatic fringe (marioecg — the biggest single discovery)

Eleven pieces, one signature: a smooth scalar field shaded like soft bas-relief, with
a razor-thin **spectral dispersion fringe living exactly on the field's iso-edges** —
nowhere else. *Mindset* (red ribbed shells, cyan fringing), *Tender* (cream ripples
around a tiny glossy chromatic pupil — a creature-eye in a calm pond), *Organa* (black
teardrop seeds each haloed magenta/cyan), *Llif* (**a matte-black goo blob with a face
emerging from boolean cutouts** — closest existing image to Moshi), *d-tach* (the
masterpiece of restraint: ONE indented circle on near-white, edge carrying a thin
full-spectrum rim).

**Steal:** our lume should not be a uniform lime glow. Lime carries the energy, but at
every membrane edge it should split — a 1-2px dispersion (lime→glow→a kiss of cyan/
magenta) *only at iso-edges, driven by the field gradient*. That one move separates
"green blob" from "optical organism." Also: marioecg works on LIGHT grounds — evidence
for a BONE-stage variant of Mosh (the "document-heavy" surface in the brief) where ink
bodies sit on bone with fringes.

## 2. Ink slabs, lume seams (hours, kaolti, junkiyoshi)

hours' *exploring booleans*: three matte-BLACK rounded slabs on off-white, separated by
razor-thin green/orange edge-light in the cuts. kaolti: **"the block is carved out by
the blob"** — a glowing soft body ERODES solid black blocks, the bite edges molten.
junkiyoshi's torus: a segmented black grub of sliced ellipsoids, white seams.

**Steal — this is the panel language for the possessed UI:** panels are not bordered
rectangles; they are **ink slabs with lume light in the seams between them**. And
panels don't slide in — **Moshi eats them out of the ink** (kaolti's erosion, edges
briefly molten, then settling to a quiet seam). "Drawers bloom" finally has a render.

## 3. Presence in fog (MCHX17, sabosugi, specoolar)

MCHX17's whole practice: full-bleed soft volumetric gradients where a figure *almost*
resolves — a red ghost looming in blue-violet haze (*Soft Continuum 2*), aurora silk,
Rothko-banded glow. sabosugi's *Inside Quantum*: **a green plasma orb with horizontal
interference streaks on pure black — literally lime-on-ink, today**. specoolar's black
hole: a void wearing a glowing accretion ring.

**Steal:** HEARTH's next iteration should be less "veins everywhere," more **presence**
— a single barely-resolved lime mass breathing in ink fog, interference streaks raking
across it (sabosugi), with the REC state as accretion (specoolar: the void itself
wearing the ember). Restraint > density.

## 4. Parameter poetry (BlousonRouge, lukasspitter, KaibaRH, Jaenam97, hive_echo, pattlas)

Six separate people are building **tools whose parameter racks are part of the art**:
the p5.steeve debug HUD (mono green `POINTS.SEED: 2 / CURVE.TENSION: 1.5` beside a
neon spline); lukasspitter's FLIP/Liquid Glass app (panel sections named CONTENT /
DISPERSION / REFLECTIONS / ABSORPTION / FRESNEL / **MOLTEN** / CAUSTICS over molten
glass type, with a keyframe timeline); KaibaRH's DITHER LAB; Jaenam97's shader rig
(Speed/Cut/Scale under a refractive gem); hive_echo's codex-built "Chromatic
Superformula Knot Garden" (sliders named *knot winding, bristle reach, pearl
coupling*); pattlas' algorithm explorer.

**Steal:** the Color Rack should read like THIS — a visible mono readout where the
labels are physical-poetic (GRIT · AIR · EPIC · MOLTEN), values tabular, sitting beside
the organism it modulates. Parameters-as-poetry is the native UI dialect of this whole
scene — and it's already lime-mono-on-black. (Also: hive_echo's practice — prompting
coding agents into 3D toys — IS our playground methodology. We're in good company.)

## 5. The diffused body (obtainer, p1xelfool, Nicolas_Sassoon, swzydzn, 314o_de_315)

obtainer: butterflies as sparse RGB-subpixel dust on black. p1xelfool: **magenta +
acid-green dendrite point-clouds on black** (one tone-swap from our palette).
Sassoon's *Shimmers*: RGB moiré weave. swzydzn: VHS scanline noise. 314o:
a pixel-ember comet streak.

**Steal:** the "AI-diffused pixel Moshi" the original research dreamed about doesn't
need diffusion — **dither/dot-dust IS the texture**. Moshi's pixel-jump state =
the SDF body re-rendered as lime point-dust (ordered dither + sparse RGB scatter),
clip distress = one frame of Sassoon moiré. Cheap, portable, on-brand.

## 6. The specimen vitrine (techartist_, Jaenam97, yamnor, eatora22, tofu logic)

techartist_: a planet sealed in a glass capsule with rainbow-fringed rim. Jaenam97:
worlds inside refractive gems. yamnor's PiMO: molecular orbitals as soft pink wobbly
blobs with a black nucleus in a friendly white tool. eatora22: real tofu wearing a
white wireframe vertex overlay — **annotation pinned to soft matter**.

**Steal:** validates 007-TERRARIUM's instinct (creature kept legible behind glass) and
names the render-layer visual: **each generative take = a small gem/capsule with the
variant world inside it**; lineage = capsules budded off the clip. And eatora22's
tofu overlay is the answer to "how do widgets attach to a gooey body" — survey-pin
annotation, not chrome.

## 7. Type that moshes (SesoHQ board, M_Pierini, enma__0312, riomadeit)

SesoHQ's board: black/red/**acid-yellow** techwear posters — heavy condensed display,
mono microtext, barcodes, glyph furniture, JP accents ("CATALYST" is practically our
palette). M_Pierini: CMYK misregistration as a system. enma__0312: a chibi sprite
**sitting inside an FL Studio EQ curve** ("I don't understand DAWs at all") — the
character-living-in-the-interface gag, proven adorable. riomadeit: a CRT playing the
WMP visualizer — "can't believe humans saw this and built Spotify" — our 2009-
screensaver nostalgia, confirmed as a living cultural reference.

**Steal:** the poster grammar (heavy display + mono microtext + glyph furniture +
registration marks) is Mosh's print voice — bring it into BROADCAST and export covers.
Moshi should physically sit ON interface curves (EQ, automation) the way enma's sprite
does. And the centerpiece really is a cultural slot people remember and miss.

## 8. Annotated simulation (IOivm, poetengineer__, blankensmithing, graceb_art)

IOivm: watercolor pigment blooming on paper WITH its own numbered vector annotations
(#1–#14, dashed arrows). poetengineer's *supremesh*: a 3×3 plate of dashed-wireframe
specimens with orange + markers — engineering notebook as art. blankensmithing: neural
cellular automata growing a self-portrait in fingerprint ridges. graceb/De Giuli:
thermal-rainbow terrain, randomness drawn from hardware.

**Steal:** the lab's own gallery should present experiments like *supremesh* — a
specimen plate with mono annotations. And NCA/reaction-diffusion is the natural
"skin texture" for Moshi's feral end (ridges grow when aggression rises).

## What this changes in our work (concrete revisions)

1. **HEARTH v2** → fewer veins, more *presence in fog* (motif 3); add gradient-driven
   spectral fringe on iso-edges (motif 1); REC-adjacent heat = accretion ring.
2. **Panel language for the possessed UI (008)** → ink slabs + lume seams; panels are
   *eaten open*, edges briefly molten (motif 2).
3. **Color Rack** → parameter-poetry mono readout (motif 4): labels like instruments,
   values tabular, sitting against the organism.
4. **Pixel state** → dither-dust render of the SDF body, not sprites (motif 5).
5. **Render layers** → takes as glass capsules/gems with worlds inside (motif 6).
6. **SPECIMEN feral skin** → reaction-diffusion ridge growth, not just spike noise
   (motif 8); eyes/widgets pin to the body like eatora22's tofu wireframe.
7. **Brand surfaces** → SesoHQ poster grammar; BONE-ground variant is real (marioecg
   works light; "Llif" proves ink-on-light reads).
