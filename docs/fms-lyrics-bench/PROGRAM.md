## Charter

Solve finish-my-song **lyrics-first**: a verifiable masked-word/infill benchmark over
known-good lyrics (word / rhyme / span / line granularities), a calibrated judging
stack, and a bake-off of arms (prompt-side, FIM-LoRA, learned ranker, combos).
Winners promote into `service/lyrics/core.py`'s `_propose_line`/`_rank` seam behind
flags. Mapping fills onto the mumble-take skeleton stays out of scope (Phase-3 sing
lane owns it).

Data posture (owner decision, 2026-07-24): personal-research; third-party lyric
text and any trained weights live only under `~/Library/Mosh/lyrics-bench/`
(licenseTier `eval-only`), never in git, never shipped. Committed artifacts carry
numbers and hashes only.

## Verdict bars — DRAFT (frozen at I2, before any arm optimization)

> These are the PROPOSED registrations. They are **not frozen yet**; I2's
> calibration sitting freezes them (edits after that point require a ledger entry
> with a reason, and never mid-arm).

- **GO (promote):** golden ≥ `product-llm` by the pre-stated effect (≥ +5 pts
  constrained-fit@1 or ≥60% trusted-judge win-rate) AND validator pass-rate ≥
  baseline AND owner blind pairwise ≥60% over ≥20 pairs AND p50 latency ≤ 1.5×
  the current llm path.
- **ITERATE:** dev-better / golden-inconclusive → max 2 cycles per arm.
- **KILL:** golden ≤ baseline, two dry rounds, ear rejects, or infeasible.
- **Golden eval budget:** 2 reads per arm; **every** golden read is appended to
  the ledger below, flattering or not. Dev is unlimited.
- **HALT rule:** no arm optimization until a judge combo reaches ≥0.65 agreement
  with the owner's blind labels (calibration sitting 1). Until then only the
  deterministic columns mean anything (the scoreboard says so).
- **Judge-health tripwire:** ≥3 of 10 sampled arm "wins" rejected by the owner →
  the arm freezes and the program returns to calibration.
- **Fame-bucket honesty:** headline comparisons use the low-views bucket
  (frontier models have famous rap in pretraining; `views` is on every item).

3. **Calibration sitting 1** (~45 min) — **READY**. Two commands:

   ```bash
   python3 service/lyrics/bench/bench_cli.py calibrate serve
   ```

   Open the printed `http://127.0.0.1:8765/`, then judge with the keyboard:
   **A** = top bar is better, **B** = bottom bar is better, **T** = too close to
   call (← goes back). Each screen shows the bars around a gap and two ways of
   filling it — one is the real recorded lyric, one is a machine's. You are not
   told which, the order is randomized per pair, and a handful of pairs repeat
   on purpose to measure your own consistency. Rate on instinct; there is no
   wrong answer. When you're done:

   ```bash
   python3 service/lyrics/bench/bench_cli.py calibrate report
   ```

   That prints which automated metric (if any) actually tracks your ear, writes
   `calibration/TRUSTED_METRICS.json`, and either lifts the HALT or keeps it.
