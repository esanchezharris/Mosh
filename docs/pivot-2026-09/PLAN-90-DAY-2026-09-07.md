## 1. Capacity (the correction the handoff asked for)

The owner **is** the engineer. Coding-agent lanes multiply drafting, not human integration,
audit or listening. One person, ≈24 h/week total, of which the owner-role hours (3 scheduled
+ 1 contingency: 90 min real recording/mixing incl. the weekly correction, 45 min blind
listening, 15 min rating/lesson, 30 min export/setup/decisions) are a **subset**, not an
addition. Effective engineering ≈ 16–18 h/week after review, repairs and the weekly
correction; unattended processing is separate. Preparation, instruction, audition, manual
finishing, debugging and rescue are logged separately; founder testing on a real song is
not "voluntary use".

The 2026-09-06 amendment restates the inherited "20 focused engineering hours/week (≈16 + 4)".
**This section governs**: 20 h is an inherited figure, not a measurement, and it double-counts
the owner-role hours that are a subset here. See conflict C6 in the reconciliation.

## 2. Iteration policy (one rule, two scopes)

An **implementation candidate** gets one bounded repair cycle after its independent audit,
then a blocked verdict and narrowed scope. A **musical experiment** gets two planned
iterations within its six-round cap. Every step includes the weekly correction (amendment
clause 4).
