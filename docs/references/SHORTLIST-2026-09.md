## Licence summary
- Abletunes: "Deconstruct and learn arrangement, structure, sound processing, automation" — explicitly what we want; samples/presets royalty-free; no redistribution of templates. The best-aligned vendor.
- Tech House Market: "100% royalty-free for personal and commercial releases", framed as educational.
- Loopmasters / BVKER: standard royalty-free sample terms; no explicit deconstruction clause (learning from a purchased set is ordinary use).
- Our use is internal measurement (device chains, gains, note stats) — no template or sample leaves the machine; stats go in `references/<genre>/stats.json`, never the sets themselves.
