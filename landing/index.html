Verse What’s inside bars 1–16

Everything a song needs. Nothing to set up.

Mosh is a native Mac app, not a browser tab. The drum kit, the synth, the tuner and the mix tools are already inside when you open it.

01

Start with a beat

One button drops a drum beat onto the built-in kit. Add a MIDI track, pick a synth preset, and write the 808 underneath it.

+ Drum beat
02

Record the vocal

Arm a track, set a count-in, and sing. Takes land on the timeline as clips you can cut, double and stack into a hook.

Count-in · 1 bar
03

Tune it and mix it

Mosh AutoTune, a compressor and a four-band EQ ship with the app. Your own VST3 and AU plugins load beside them and open in their own windows.

+ Add plugin
04

Re-Imagine a take

Select a clip, describe where it should go, and Mosh renders a new version of that take on your own Mac. Audition it, then keep it or throw it away.

Runs locally
Hook Moshi bars 17–24

Say it. Moshi does it.

Type what you want in plain words. Moshi makes the change with the same commands your own clicks use, so it shows up in History and one undo takes it back.

  • Pleased A laugh when your change lands.
  • Asleep Curls into a ball while you record, so nothing distracts the take.
Bridge Two-player bars 25–32

Two Macs. One song.

Start a session, send a room code, and you are both inside the same project, each on your own machine.

you
room code
friend

Invite a friend

Each of you takes a track, and a small lock shows who is editing what. The song syncs between you. Your voices don’t, so keep a call open on the side.

Your phone is a pad Early

Scan a code with the iPhone camera and a controller for the session opens in Safari. There is no app to install.
